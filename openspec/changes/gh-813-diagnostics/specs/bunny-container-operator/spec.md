## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mac-local diagnostics stack
The repository SHALL define one owned Compose project, `bunny-diagnostics` (`deploy/diagnostics/compose.yaml`), with an OpenTelemetry Collector, Loki, Tempo and Grafana pinned by multi-architecture image digests, that the operator starts and stops by hand beside the runtime (Hub #813). It SHALL publish only Grafana on `127.0.0.1:3000` and the Collector's OTLP/HTTP receiver on `127.0.0.1:4318`, on its own bridge network that the runtime never joins; the runtime SHALL reach the Collector through `host.docker.internal`. Logs SHALL reach Loki and spans Tempo on one OTLP path each, with the Collector copying the event name to the queryable `event_name` attribute, Loki's stream labels limited to the contract's namespace, service and environment and everything else structured metadata. Grafana SHALL be provisioned with the Loki and Tempo sources, a `trace_id` derived field that opens the trace and a traces-to-logs query that finds the trace's records, and the starter dashboard; it SHALL be reached without a password on the Mac's loopback, as the trusted-loopback dashboard sign-in is, with sign-up, the login form, analytics and plugin fetches off. Loki and Tempo SHALL retain seven days with their compactors and SHALL bound admission (Loki 1 MiB/s with a 4 MiB burst and 16 KB lines; Tempo 2 MiB/s with 5 MiB per trace); the Collector SHALL retry a refusing backend for at most 30 seconds and then drop; every container SHALL have a memory limit, no restart policy, a read-only root, dropped capabilities and no new privileges. The three data volumes SHALL hold diagnostic data only, which `down -v` may delete; nothing SHALL back up, restore, spool or replay it, and no Collector or runtime failure SHALL change an application result.

#### Scenario: A synthetic record and span reach the viewer
- **WHEN** a disposable copy of the project starts on alternate loopback ports and one profile 1.7 record and one span with the same trace ID are posted to the Collector
- **THEN** Grafana's health is `ok`, both datasources and the starter dashboard are provisioned, Loki answers the trace-ID query with the record's labels and structured metadata, and Tempo returns the span, and `down -v` leaves no container, network or volume of that project

#### Scenario: The operating procedure
- **WHEN** the operator follows the container guide's "Diagnostics stack" section
- **THEN** the pinned images are pulled once, the project is created, started, inspected and stopped with the documented commands, actual volume usage is read with Docker's accounting, and loss is read from the Collector's log and the runtime's `telemetry.dropped` and `runtime.stopped` records
