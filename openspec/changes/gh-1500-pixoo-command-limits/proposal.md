## Why

Earlier physical trials demonstrated two and twenty raw frames and five hundred hosted frames. These sample sizes currently prevent the selected direct Pixoo from playing ordinary eight-frame art. Divoom documents a smaller-than-60 field on the direct command and dimensions on the hosted command. [Issue #1500](https://github.com/jimmie-potts/agent-device-hub/issues/1500)

## What Changes

- Enforce the raw command’s 59-frame maximum before a write. Preserve the configured route and single physical frame delay.
- Admit whole GIFs, imports and previews according to their measured resource use. Keep all frames and the source timing.
- Preserve existing originals, renditions, playlists and configuration names. Distinguish published command constraints from earlier physical observations in settings and refusals.
