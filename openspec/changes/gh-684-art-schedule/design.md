## Context

See proposal.md for the motivation. The baseline is the current TypeScript runtime, not the legacy Hub automation routes named in the issue's historical assessment. `AutomationPart` already owns current device copies, session attention, `AutomationStore`, tracked dispatch, quiet hours and moment budgets. `power-schedules.ts` supplies the existing host-local, future-only scheduling pattern. `LivePublication` prevents replayed core occurrences from acquiring effect authority. No second scheduler service, installation or device writer is needed.

The shared-art implementation in `art-plans.ts` exposes `art.targets()` for configured display IDs/kinds and `art.present(plan, permit)`. Consume its accepted version before product work; the scheduler does not replace its preparation, ownership or result handling. Main now supplies both exact-art display consumers, while the shared-art interface remains a separately reviewed input. Its required synchronous permit checks caller policy immediately before each dispatch. Preparation, browser selection and moment results remain separate. The Art owner retains originals, compositions, provider accounting and ambient selection. Gallery browsing has its own local manual selection; Home layout pinning is placement, not evidence of a physical display pin.

Weather publishes a closed `weather-state/2.0` record. `current` is an hourly forecast, not a measurement; `likely` distinguishes a likely condition from a chance. Freshness ends at `staleAtMs`. `promptWords()` also includes a temperature, so it cannot be copied wholesale to an art prompt. No shipped calendar producer or calendar source grant exists. Art provenance is deliberately `factual: false`.

This design is required by the schema: it crosses state owners and versioned messages, adds private durable claims, and changes arbitration and visible presentation. ADR 0012 owns command/error/outcome semantics; ADR 0011 and the diagnostic contract own private evidence. No retained controller 1.x contract changes.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** keep opportunity selection, paid creation, free preparation, browser selection, display transmission and physical observation separate. Fit bounded art policy into the current automation owner and expose it through the existing Automation workspace. Make every suppression and uncertain result inspectable without turning admission into success.

**Non-Goals:** a cron platform, timezone service, calendar connector, arbitrary event-ingestion API, generic notification payload, new personal source, automatic device mode changes, wallpaper/occupancy features, or inferred task completion. No provider text becomes an operational fact. Source delivery does not install or contact a display.

## Decisions

### One art policy beside existing automation settings

Add `ArtSchedule` under `AutomationPart`, with a focused `art-schedule.ts` helper and synchronous private store operations. Keep legacy event rules, routines and `AutomationSettings` payloads unchanged. A separate small `/api/v2/automation/art` branch in `automation-routes.ts` uses the same read/control scopes, `bunny-request:1`, Origin checks, bounded JSON and post-body authorization. Do not add a page or a second connection.

The closed policy contains enabled, host-local waking start/end, appearances, selected configured display IDs and browser participation, ambient enabled, optional generation, weather use and explicit art interrupt permission. Appearance choices are 6, 7 or 8, with 7 the default. A new store seeds a disabled draft once, with suggested 08:00 to 22:00 hours and generation off until explicitly selected. Enabling is an explicit owner action; installation selects the actual hours. An equal waking start/end is invalid; a crossing-midnight interval belongs to its starting local date. Updates carry the observed policy revision, so two tabs cannot silently overwrite one another. Old event-settings writes cannot erase art policy.

The existing global quiet hours apply even when they use their already-supported explicit zone. Art's waking schedule itself uses only the host local clock. The UI displays that zone. Do not add time zone configuration or location inference.

### Bounded daily opportunities and durable claims

Plan at most eight appearance opportunities for one waking interval, including an early, middle and late window when the interval can fit them. Use an injected bounded choice function to vary times, and persist the chosen instants with that interval's policy revision. The owning admission/planning rule requires each new appearance's earliest start to be at least 45 elapsed minutes after the preceding claim's latest permitted start, `startAtMs + toleranceMs`. Persist that conservative bound across targets, restart and day boundaries; it is a permitted-start bound, not an observed physical start. A short interval produces fewer opportunities and an explanation, not compressed spacing. Quiet hours remove opportunities. The day plan is a bounded operational projection, not retained source history.

Use the runtime scheduler and integer delays. Re-evaluate on relevant owner state, policy changes and scheduled wakeups; no browser timer or Hub polling drives effects. Recompute a future plan after a host-clock jump, local-day change or policy edit while retaining claim watermarks. A repeated local hour cannot duplicate a claimed slot; a missing local time is skipped. Restart skips every due/past slot and creates only future opportunities. Policy revisions change future eligibility, not the identity or count of already claimed work for that waking day. Disable/re-enable, count or waking-hour edits cannot mint another daily allowance. Keep the greatest admitted interval watermark; a clock rollback into an older interval cannot reopen it. Do not refill missed daily counts or defer yesterday's gifts.

A durable opportunity ID and request IDs are allocated before any paid or display request. Claims distinguish generation requested, appearance reserved and individual target attempts. Claim and count reservations commit synchronously under the core lease, before effects; no transaction crosses an await. Count a reserved appearance once across its surfaces, including uncertain/lost replies. A crash can lose a gift but cannot authorize a replacement attempt. Keep current interval counters and the preceding claim's latest permitted start across day boundaries so midnight cannot defeat 45-minute spacing. Share the existing global/device budget accounting with ordinary automation in that same synchronous admission. The current engine counts handed flourishes after awaited target reads; it does not yet count pending art reservations. Extend its owning admission/store path so ordinary flourishes also see art reservations and recheck immediately before handoff. Keep ordinary event policy and its existing per-agent/task meaning; do not invent an agent or task identity for scheduled art. Using event priority for owner-approved art status coverage cannot bypass art's shared global/device accounting. Decorative art also honors `noFlourishes`; an informative appearance still requires its enabled rain policy, fresh facts and the same art budget. Daily appearance count and provider dollars remain separate limits.

Bind each appearance reservation to its opportunity, immutable presentation plan, policy revision, budget window and selected target attempts. Shared budget admission includes pending reservations as well as counted handoffs, so unrelated automation cannot spend capacity already reserved for art. Final permits validate that exact reservation rather than asking for another unit: the appearance's own counted unit cannot exhaust its permission, and its own spacing marker cannot block its reserved targets. Other claims remain in the count and spacing checks. A reservation for another opportunity, target, composition or plan cannot authorize this attempt. This distinction does not grant ambient work a takeover reservation or turn a browser selection into a device handoff.

In the final synchronous permit, require the original live claim, its matching plan and unconsumed target attempt, with current limits still admitting that reservation. For a count limit, usage including this reservation may equal the limit; a new unrelated claim requires room for an additional unit. Reuse the owning spacing rule against other claims, excluding the matching reservation's own start bound so its targets cannot block themselves. Apply current policy revocation, quiet hours, attention and expiry even to reserved work. Record the target handoff once before returning permission; later calls cannot reuse it. A denied or lost handoff remains conservatively counted and is not refunded into a replacement or replay after restart. No lookup, check or claim mutation crosses an await.

At most one appearance evaluation and one coalesced ambient opportunity wait. A new weather change replaces the pending ambient candidate; it does not enqueue history. Hourly ambient opportunities and live weather changes never request a takeover. A weather change coinciding with an appearance shares that idea rather than doubling generation. Suppressed work may wait only inside its original window; the final start/cutoff never moves once a presentation plan has been admitted.

### Generation is preparation, not permission to show

Choose one artwork and its independently readable compositions, preferring saved favorites/positive feedback and excluding dismissed choices. Use the bounded current Art catalog copy; absence of a usable composition is a per-target limitation, not permission to invent an ID. A deterministic injected selector makes reuse testable and does not treat silence as feedback.

When generation is enabled, one early bounded preparation phase may request one automatic job for an opportunity, through `art-auto`. Save its request ID before sending. Read the producer's job result, not merely the command admission. If new art is unavailable or misses the selection deadline, choose saved art once; if none exists, record a skipped opportunity. A later job may enrich the gallery but cannot resurrect this opportunity. Automatic generation uses the existing `action:start` with `purpose:appearance` and its effect-free completion, extended only with explicit weather use. It must not use the old `purpose:ambient` path that can select a tile after asynchronous work without renewed schedule permission. No new generation purpose or parallel job lifecycle is needed.

The Art ledger remains the sole dollar accountant. The scheduler never copies, resets or grants around provider ceilings, daily pace, the automatic loop guard, lost-response reservations or no-retry policy. Saved reuse and local notice treatment call no provider. Pause of generation leaves saved art and the existing ambient tile usable.

### Dispatch through the existing presentation owner

`art.targets()` supplies configured `{target, kind}` identities, including temporarily unavailable owners. Map composition targets to those records; never assume a literal device ID. Call `art.present({id, artworkId, startAtMs, prepareUntilMs, toleranceMs, mood, priorityClass, coversStatus, displays, browser}, permit)`. Bind the synchronous `permit(surface)` closure to that immutable plan and its reservation. It checks the current enabled policy/revision, claimed opportunity, expiry, quiet hours, budgets and spacing with that reservation recognized, active attention and any current owner hold immediately before each dispatch. The shared controller separately checks current configured ownership, fresh SDK membership, capabilities, generation and tracked preparation success, then rechecks activity and time after the permit.

The shared controller durably binds `plan.id` to the artwork, compositions, targets, durations, start, preparation cutoff, tolerance, mood, priority and status permission before any phase. Changing those values under the same ID returns `duplicate-conflict`, including after restart. Its receipt is not an executable schedule or a substitute for the opportunity reservation. Never reschedule an old ID to borrow late preparation; only a separately eligible future opportunity can receive a new ID and its own budget claim.

Call the presentation API only within its existing 60-second start horizon; earlier generation preparation remains a separate phase. The preparation cutoff is no later than the fixed start, and the admitted plan never moves to accommodate a late stage. Use separate durations with defaults of 10 seconds for Pixoo and 30 seconds for Tidbyt, a fixed start and bounded preparation cutoff. The Tidbyt default follows the owner's 30-to-60-second appearance range and the shared controller's 30-second minimum. Each request must fit the target's current declared capability; skip a target that cannot accept its selected duration instead of shortening Tidbyt below 30 seconds or bypassing its bound. One missing or late device does not delay another. Only the owner's accepted art interrupt policy can set `coversStatus`; no flourish covers status. Store that permission explicitly in the art policy instead of silently broadening the existing generic interrupt set. Device pause/pin/Now Playing/attention admission remains final; empty Media and stopped or finished saved-item playback are eligible under the accepted Pixoo rules; an explicit pause, an uncertain writer or a stale playback generation is not. Neither an empty playlist nor a saved item changes those owner protections. Send no mode command and restore no captured base snapshot.

The result contains `tracked` request IDs or `not-requested` typed errors for each preparation/moment/browser attempt. Join tracked records using target, family and requester before presenting their live outcome. Browser completion means selected reference committed, not decoded or visible. Only the transient ArtPresentation provider supplies decoded-visible evidence. Preserve manual Gallery selection; schedule updates never change its local selection or the Home layout.

### Free ambient selection stays with Art

Extend the current `art-auto/2.1` with a closed `2.2` action for one exact saved Tidbyt composition and configured target. It stages through the existing tracked path and then uses the Art owner's serialized ambient selection/renewal machinery. It never creates a job, calls a provider, writes Pixoo Media or starts a moment. Browser ambient selection uses the existing exact browser selection path; theme follows decoded presentation.

Ambient selection needs a final policy check after staging, not just at command admission. Carry a narrow opportunity authorization reference in a closed `art-dispatch/2.1` ambient alternative; retain the existing 2.0 stage/ambient payloads unchanged. The core resolves the reference only against the current in-memory art opportunity and runs its permit immediately before forwarding the ambient command. Scope this reference to one target, composition and attempt, consume it once, and drop it on stop/restart/policy revocation. It grants no stage, moment or other family. The Art owner retains its own serialized last-issued intent, definite-refusal rollback and uncertain-result semantics. A rejected/revoked final handoff does not replace the preceding issued tile. Do not store a callback, replay a grant or introduce a reusable authorization framework.

Separate a pending scheduled selection from the durable renewable intent. Existing Art staging selects its new stage before dispatch, while renewal reads that selected stage and a definite ambient refusal restores only the last-issued record. Reusing that sequence unchanged could let a refused new stage renew later. For the new scheduled path, a pending, revoked, restart-lost or definitely refused selection cannot replace the preceding renewable intent. Persist the issued intent's exact composition and configured target before its handoff; retain an uncertain handoff as issued without claiming acceptance or retrying that attempt. A definite refusal leaves the preceding renewable intent intact.

The one-use grant authorizes a new selection, not maintenance of an already-issued intent. Accepted or uncertain issued intent retains Art's existing local renewal lifecycle across schedule disable and restart, subject to current target-owner protections. Maintenance stays bound to that exact composition and configured target; it cannot borrow a pending selection's identity or retarget another device. Disabling scheduling revokes unissued opportunities and grants, without automatically clearing the existing tile. Explicit deletion and a later admitted replacement retain their existing meaning. Renewal is not a retry of an uncertain selection attempt, and introduces no provider call, generation or catch-up opportunity.

### Closed factual notice outside the staged image

Add a rain-forecast notice to `moment-play/2.2`, valid only beside an exact `art` reference, and advertise support under `device/2.3` as a closed `moments.art.notices` list. These are the next minor versions in the inspected shared-art implementation; confirm their availability before implementation. Extend the existing shared-art plan with this optional closed notice, include it in its durable identity digest, and pass it to existing moment dispatch. A plain-art plan still sends 2.1. Do not add a second factual-presentation controller. Existing schemas remain unchanged and old readers never receive fields from a newer schema. All new art-bearing alternatives retain core-only tracker admission and `artCommandRefusal` at the receiving owner. A target without this notice capability is explicitly skipped for a factual appearance; do not silently strip its label and claim the notice showed.

The notice is a closed rain-only object: a rain-forecast discriminant, likely/chance, forecast start/end, provider forecast-update time, observation time, stale time and weather source revision. All instants and revisions are bounded safe integers. Source identity is fixed to the qualified weather owner. No arbitrary label, event body, location, title, temperature or provider string crosses this boundary. Use a fresh forecast hour in a bounded approaching window (the next two hours), not the occurrence's words as evidence. Recheck source freshness, hour coverage and opportunity relevance before every factual dispatch. Require notice validity through the latest permitted start plus that target's bounded hold; if it cannot remain valid, skip that factual target. Devices recheck these time bounds at admission and before their first notice write, including a delayed queue. The core checks the latest source revision before handoff; it cannot retract a transmission already sent if a later forecast changes.

Each display renders the exact saved image in a dedicated artwork region and a separate fixed high-contrast notice band. It never edits stored bytes or reuses a composition ID for altered bytes. On 64×32, allocate two readable fixed-font lines for forecast/likelihood and the host-local hour, with the remaining area showing a fitted decorative image; on 64×64 use the same meaning at its readable size. Fixed text derives from enums and times. Show "Forecast" and distinguish "Chance" from likely rain. Do not turn an NWS forecast into "rain observed," an official safety alert, or a provider-generated factual scene. Inspect actual pixel fixtures at native size.

The browser renders a separate accessible label and evidence details beside the exact decoded visible artwork. Core `art-schedule/2.0` state supplies policy, bounded opportunity evidence, the current notice, its opportunity/artwork/composition IDs, expiry and operation references through the existing SDK sync/live path. It owns these derived facts, not a second catalog. Publish them through the core's existing transaction/outbox path, with unavailable status after startup until required copies are fresh. The browser shows a notice only after the matching browser-selection operation succeeded and that composition is decoded and visible. Matching image IDs alone are insufficient when a new opportunity reuses the same image. A browser notice lasts at most 30 seconds from the admitted start and never beyond source or forecast validity; it does not restore an older image afterward. Loading, hidden art, replacement, expiry, stale source or disabled policy clears the current label, while the saved decorative image may remain. Generated catalog provenance stays `factual: false`; the notice is explicitly a deterministic forecast annotation.

### Source enablement and private evidence

Only the enabled schedule-weather path opens its SDK copy and live occurrence subscription. Disabling closes them, clears derived opportunity evidence and causes zero subsequent schedule source reads. Do not persist a weather-owner state copy; persist only the core's own claim/result and the fact it used, under private storage. The independently configured weather module and existing Art follower retain their own authority; a schedule toggle is not a claim to stop their separate collection.

Automatic `art-auto/2.2` starts carry explicit current/omit weather-use policy. `omit` makes no job weather read and supplies no weather prompt words. `current` permits only fresh closed condition words, rechecked by Art before every paid call. Do not pass temperature from `promptWords`, forecast labels, notice evidence or personal events into model prompts. Existing owner-request semantics remain unchanged.

Meeting presentation is outside this release after the owner's 2026-11-05 decision. Compose no calendar reader, adapter, source grant, placeholder switch or unavailable-only meeting feature. The later calendar/presentation work needs an accepted source and personal-source permission. A turn-ended event is not meeting, milestone or success evidence. Source controls in this change expose only the implemented weather use, with disabled/unavailable/stale states and zero reads by disabled schedule consumers.

Use existing operation IDs, fixed error codes and registered diagnostic fields. Carry the existing trace context through scheduled claim, paid-job request, preparation and each presentation attempt; link source occurrences without logging their payloads. Record fixed suppression codes and neutral opportunity/request IDs in the private automation history, not arbitrary weather words or labels in diagnostics. New policy/notice refusal uses `invalid-state`, `unsupported-capability`, `unavailable`, `expired` before intake, or `cancelled`/the applicable failed outcome after intake; preserve the missed-start classification established by the display owners. Do not publish private labels or collected evidence to GitHub. Reuse private history for retained facts and bounded operational state for current UI; a bounded UI projection is not a new deletion policy.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Fewer than the requested daily appearances] → Quiet hours, a short waking interval, unavailable targets and owner control win; expose suppression rather than catching up or compressing spacing.
- [Policy changes while generation or staging waits] → Separate generation from presentation, check permits at each actual dispatch, revoke the narrow ambient grant and keep device-owner checks final.
- [A claimed attempt is lost in a crash] → Count it conservatively and never resend. A later unclaimed window can still be evaluated independently.
- [Factual text overwhelms a tiny panel] → Fixed vocabulary, a dedicated readable band, fitted unchanged artwork and native-size inspection; no generated text or scrolling caption dependency.
- [Minor version differences across consumers] → Old schemas remain exact, new capability gates new payloads, and packaged real-consumer fixtures assert both acceptance and refusal.
- [Forecast changes after a permitted transmission] → Bound each factual hold and check current source before handoff; clear browser evidence when it changes, without claiming that sent pixels can be recalled.
- [Deferred meeting presentation] → Ship rain only, with no calendar access or placeholder UI. A later pickup must establish accepted source and permission; no synthetic fixture substitutes for source delivery.

## Migration Plan

Before product edits, document owning build/type/test/browser/scenario coverage and CI discovery. Reconcile the final accepted shared-art interfaces and display changes into this isolated branch. Add new private art policy/claim tables under the existing lease without rewriting legacy rules, Art originals or device stores. Seed only once; invalid stored art policy refuses scheduled work with a visible failure instead of silently enabling defaults.

Ship schemas and consumers together, retaining all older schema readers. Source checks cover store reopen, old settings compatibility, disabled policy and partial module availability. Installation enables the policy only through the existing authenticated owner path after the selected integrated release is qualified. Disabling stops future scheduling and revokes unsent work; no command replay or snapshot restore is a rollback mechanism. Installed behavior and optical accuracy require the separate deployment owner's evidence.

## Validation approach

- Drive an entire synthetic waking day with injected clock/choice and temporary SQLite. Assert morning/day/evening, 45-minute spacing after the prior latest permitted start, quiet windows, crossing midnight, clock jumps, restart and fewer opportunities in a short day. Reopen the same store to prove durable deduplication and conservative counts.
- Hold real consumer preparation and operation outcomes while changing policy, device generation, attention and source freshness. Assert the actual command trace contains no forbidden late dispatch, resend or mode write, and that one slow device does not move another's start. Exercise unknown response and storage-fault branches, not only normal completion.
- Reserve the last allowed budget unit, hold preparation, and admit competing ordinary automation and another art opportunity. Only the original reserved attempts may proceed; their own count/spacing markers do not block them, and an unrelated attempt cannot borrow or refund the reservation. Revoke policy before one target's permit and verify that target stays unsent while the conservative count remains. Assert 30-second Tidbyt and 10-second Pixoo requests, capability refusal, and conflict on changed timing under an old plan ID.
- Reuse real saved synthetic compositions with provider-call counters at zero. Hold an ambient stage across revocation and restart; assert the one-use authorization is rejected and the previous issued tile survives. After issuing A, disable scheduling and advance through renewal and restart; A remains the exact renewable intent without reusing its grant. Then hold replacement B through revocation or definite refusal and advance through renewal/restart; A remains renewable and B is never selected. Preserve uncertain issued evidence without retrying its attempt. Verify omitted weather performs zero schedule/job reads and never enters generated prompts.
- Test both old closed schemas and the additive payload/capability combinations through packaged event-contract consumers. Render chance and likely rain notices at native 64×32/64×64; inspect readability, fixed semantics, unchanged saved hashes, no unlabelled fallback and ordinary art without notices.
- Extend the existing Art/Automation browser checks at 320, 390 and 1440px, light/dark and reduced motion. Exercise keyboard focus, read-only/stale policy, two-tab conflict, lost mutation reply, delayed decode, repeated identical art with a failed new selection, exact selected identity, notice expiry, Gallery manual choice and unchanged Home geometry.
- Add one finite offline runtime scenario using the shipped synthetic provider and both real display owners' simulated transports. Its marked walkthrough must show schedule enablement, an appearance with mixed per-target evidence, a fresh forecast notice and policy revocation during delayed preparation. Independent acceptance must inspect the full recording, native render fixtures, artifact hashes and cleanup. It makes no installed or optical claim.
- Before implementation, add the coverage and selector routing to `docs/development.md` and CI. Run root build before lint, then typecheck, event/contract/package consumers, Art, weather, runtime, dashboard, Tidbyt and Pixoo owning suites, focused scenario/browser checks and workflow checks. Preserve unrelated failures with their exact evidence. Independent Standards/Specification, focused persistence/device-safety review and disposable acceptance apply to the committed comparison.

## Alternatives and scope boundary

The owner settled the factual scope as rain notices, with meetings deferred. The task list can now be completed; it grants no implementation authority while the coordinator is still integrating the selected source inputs. GitHub owns that prerequisite status.

Reusing `art.present` keeps preparation, target identity, fixed timing and tracked results with their current owner. A second scheduler service or direct transport path would duplicate those protections. A narrow ambient authorization reference is needed because Art serializes selection after asynchronous staging; an admission-only policy check could paint after revocation. It is one-use and local to this feature, not a general permission framework.

Closed minor wire versions are needed for factual labels, explicit weather omission and the free ambient action because old schemas reject extra fields. New families for individual notice types, a generic event bus, provider-generated factual text and a calendar placeholder add no first-release capability. The one new `art-schedule` state family is justified by the existing frontend needing authoritative policy and expiring evidence without a polling route or a second Art owner.
