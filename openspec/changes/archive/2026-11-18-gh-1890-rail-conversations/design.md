## Context

The rail (#1887), the palette (#1888), the Devices menu and the pinned pages (#1889) are in place. The Conversation page is a companions frontend page that the shell mounts in one module scope; it owns the command control and the one speech session. The shell already reaches companion code through `@jimmie-potts/companions/frontend`: the page contribution and the two Home widgets, each mounted in its own module scope. The design note on the issue settled the seam before code, and its six defaults stand.

## Decisions

- The companions frontend exports one more component, `ConversationRail`, and one hook, `useConversationWaiting`. The shell mounts the component in the rail's bottom group through `ModuleFrontend`, in its own module scope, with no control authority and no operations. It is mounted while the catalog declares the Conversation page of a running companions module. The shell imports nothing else of the module's conversation, and no SDK type changes.
- The selected profile lives in a small in-memory store inside the companions frontend (`conversation-rail.ts`). A rail row sets it and links to the Conversation page's existing address; the page reads it instead of its own state. The page also tells the store while it is mounted, so the rail marks the open conversation without knowing any route. A profile has no address of its own: back and forward move between pages, a reload starts on B.U.N.N.Y., and a modified click opens the page elsewhere and chooses nothing here.
- Drafts stay page state, as before: kept for each profile while the page is open and lost when it is left. Choosing a profile in the rail while the page is open keeps the page mounted, so its drafts, its command owner and its speech session stay as they are.
- The marker means the owner must answer or approve. The `assistant-state` record says that a task waits on an unanswered question but not what kind of question; only saved history does. The rail group syncs `assistant-state` through its own scope, read-only, and for a pending question reads that profile's recent history once, with the reader the page uses. A clarification or an approval is marked. A delegation, which waits on the coding worker, is not. The same test as the page's answer box decides what is pending: a task that is waiting or paused with a question and no answer.
- The open Conversation page has already read its own profile's history. It shares each question's kind through the store, and the group does not read a profile the open page is showing. Opening the page therefore costs no second read, and a kind the page learned stays known after the page is left.
- Nothing is claimed without evidence. With no live state copy (not connected, sync refused, failed, removed, restarted, or the assistant unavailable) there is no marker. Until the kind is read, and when the read fails or returns another question, there is no marker. The read is an effect of the pending question and the state revision, so it is never repeated on a timer. A read that failed is made again when the state copy next changes or is live again, or when the open Conversation page stops showing that profile; each of those changes what the effect depends on. A retry after a reconnect is wanted: a read can fail because the connection dropped.
- The phone menu button carries the marker when a conversation other than the open one needs the owner. The button keeps its name, "Open navigation", and says the rest in its description, so nothing that finds the button by name changes.
- The rail draws the group with its own classes, as it draws every row: the group's heading, a row's tile, its name, its Demo label and the marker. The companions component emits that markup and ships no stylesheet for it, so the full rail, the icon rail and the drawer stay the shell's to lay out. A row is as wide as the rail and no wider; where a font is too wide for the name and the Demo label on one line, the label moves under the name inside the same row, and neither is cut.
- The marker is a dot on the profile's tile in every rail state, so a waiting demo keeps its Demo label. The words "Needs your answer" are in the row's name, and the icon rail shows the name, the Demo label and the need beside the tile on hover or focus, as it shows a destination's name.
- On the Conversation page the Devices row is marked without a box, as on a page with its own pinned row, so the rail boxes one row: the open conversation.
- Settings and the feed indicator share one row in the foot of the full rail and the drawer; in the icon rail, which is one icon wide, the indicator sits under Settings. The indicator is an icon in every rail state, as it already was in the icon rail; its name stays its accessible name and is the first line of its details, so the feed's condition is still readable. The control-mode line stays under the row. This takes 52px off the foot.
- The line under the Pinned heading, "Pin a device page to keep it one click away.", is removed. The owner found it redundant on 2026-11-18: the header toggle on a page already says "Pin to rail". With nothing to list the heading stands alone. The rail still counts the room for one row when none is laid out (`ROW` in `rail.tsx`, the row's 36px minimum), so opening a page that shows a temporary row never changes whether the rail fits its window. The shell journey checks that an empty list needs exactly what one pinned row needs, which ties that number to a real row.
- In a window too short for the rail, the rail scrolls with the page and its parts stay together at the top. The pinned pages take their own height there and do not stretch, so the conversations and the foot sit directly under them. Since #1889 the pinned pages had stretched down the page in that state and left the foot at the page's end; the review of this change found it, and with the taller rail it shows in more windows.
- The Conversation page keeps one header row. The shell's page title stays the page's heading and is not drawn; the conversation's row shows the profile's name, the Demo label for a demo and Details. The line "Demo profile. No paid calls." stays under it. The "Companion pages" disclosure is removed: the Devices menu lists those pages.
- `--conversation-measure` is 920px. An assistant reply keeps its own 46rem line length inside it.

## Measured

On a Mac the composer was 689.75px wide at 1024px and 760px at 1280px and 1440px. It is now 884.75px at 1024px and 920px at 1280px and 1440px. The conversation group is 134px tall in the full rail. With the conversations the full rail needs 709px on a Mac to stay in view, where it needed 611px, and 727px in the Linux test container, where the control-mode line wraps onto a second line. The icon rail needs 713px in both. A rail without the companions module needs 559px on a Mac.

## Risks and tradeoffs

The rail is taller: with the conversations it needs about 100px more than before to stay in view, so it scrolls with the page in shorter windows. The pinned list still gives up its room first, down to its heading and one row. The feed's name is no longer drawn beside its icon in the full rail. A profile cannot be bookmarked or opened in a new tab by itself. While the open page's own history read is in flight, its row has no marker yet. Conversation previews, unread counts, new profiles and a thread model are deferred on the issue.
