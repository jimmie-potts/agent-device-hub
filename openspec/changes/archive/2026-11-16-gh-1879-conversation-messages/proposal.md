## Why

Conversation gives routine controls and message cards more visual weight than the reply. The saved prototype puts dialogue first and keeps supporting evidence available on demand. [Hub #1879](https://github.com/jimmie-potts/agent-device-hub/issues/1879)

## What changes

- Align open assistant replies and modest owner bubbles with the composer.
- Keep Read aloud and full saved tool results available through quiet, labelled controls.
- Offer a short invitation in a fresh empty history when input is available. Show a compact completion cue and keep decisions and unresolved outcomes visible.

## Capabilities

### Modified capabilities

- `bunny-assistant-tasks` covers the presentation of saved messages, fresh empty history and task completion.

## Impact

The companions frontend, its guide and the existing dashboard browser journeys change.
