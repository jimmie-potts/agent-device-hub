## Why

Home's Wispr widget repeats detailed collection text and has no quiet selectable daily view. The owner approved Quiet line and requested a reusable dashboard component in [Hub #1200](https://github.com/jimmie-potts/agent-device-hub/issues/1200).

## What Changes

- Export a module-owned presentation card and adapt existing numeric reads to it.
- Show words, speaking minutes and seven reporting dates with local accessible day selection and missing-date gaps.
- Keep Refresh, Open Wispr and compact truthful loading/stale/unavailable indicators.
- Preserve Home placement, exposure, privacy and the full analytics page.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `wispr-dashboard`: reusable Quiet line Home summary with accessible daily inspection and truthful compact states.

## Impact

Module browser entry, Home widget presentation, module tests and existing Wispr browser journey. No backend/API/collector/store or dependency changes. Live activation remains #1035; no source paths or settings are selected.
