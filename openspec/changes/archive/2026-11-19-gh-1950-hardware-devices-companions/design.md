## Context

The rail (#1887), the jump palette (#1888), the Devices menu with pins (#1889) and the conversations (#1890) are in place. The directory projected every module and its pages from the catalog and the device copies (`directoryEntries`), and the menu, the palette and the pins were built from it. The owner selected a device-only prototype for the Devices popout and directory after #1950 was written; it is kept privately as a reference and is not copied into the repository. Its README records decisions made after it: Companions takes Habitat's slot, the conversations stay in the rail, its LIFX names are examples, and the material comes from #1929.

## Decisions

- Hardware is decided per module: it serves device records, holds some in the page, or is a known hardware module by name, so a module that is down and serves nothing keeps its place and its state. Everything else (companions, art, Wispr, Music, presence, test fixtures) is left out of Devices.
- The categories are the prototype's: Displays, Lighting and Appliances, in that order. The menu has Displays in its first column and Lighting with Appliances in the second, a head with its own close button, lists that scroll, and "All devices" kept at its foot. Beside the rail it opens level with the Devices row, no lower than two fifths of the window.
- A lighting brand is a disclosure (a `menuitem` button with `aria-expanded`) over its lights and then its overview, as in the prototype. Collapsed lights are not rendered, so the arrow keys skip them without special cases. The menu is mounted each time it opens, so the brands start collapsed and the brand holding the open page starts expanded. The right and left arrows expand and collapse.
- Each hardware module is one entry when it has one destination. Pixoo and Tidbyt therefore have one entry each, their device pages. A module with several devices that is not lighting (Wemo's plugs) stays a heading over its devices, because each plug has only its own page; the prototype's single Wemo entry assumed a page the runtime does not have.
- Task pages stay inside the device pages: the existing tabs over a module's pages. Pixoo's tabs are mapped to the prototype's views in the shell (`feature-navigation.ts`): Display for the device page, linking Player as Advanced playback and Settings as Device settings, Media over Library and Playlists, and Agent status for Monitor. The map uses only declared pages, and a declared page it does not name becomes a tab of its own. Tidbyt's only module page shows the same card as its device page, so Tidbyt has no tabs. The display pages are titled with the display's name and their tabs sit under the heading, as the prototype has them.
- A light's page leads back to its brand; every other page under Devices leads back to All devices, and the pages under Companions to Companions.
- `destinations.ts` projects every declared page and device page with its module's name, state and hint. The jump palette offers the hardware first, then the other pages, and the pins name their rows from the same projection. Audio is a fixed hub entry under Home in both.
- Device names come from `device-names.ts`: the record's `label`, then the Nanoleaf kind from the module's `nanoleaf-wall` record, then the ID read as words. Two that would collide are told apart by their IDs. In source, LIFX and Nanoleaf device records carry no label and the Pixoo record carries the optional configured label, so the installed names of the Lines, Panels and LIFX fixtures come from the observed kind and the IDs; installed readback confirms them.
- Companions is a new route, `#/companions`, with an overview built from the catalog. Habitat and the companions module's pages select Companions; Audio, the visualizer and the Music source pages select Home. A destination over a page with its own rail row is marked lightly, as the Devices row was, so the Conversation page marks Companions lightly.

- The glass layer of #1929 is mounted once on the page's `main` while the open page is the directory, the Companions overview or a display page (`glass-page.tsx`), so it stays mounted between those pages and is released on any other. It draws the Quiet glass wash, with no backdrop and no fusing: Home alone owns those. Each card is wrapped in an element marked as a panel, because the layer's stylesheet clears the card surfaces inside a panel. A display's device card and a display's module page are one panel each; other module pages keep their layout. Veils mark the heading, section headings, notices, the breadcrumb and the view pages under the tabs, a handful per page under the layer's 32. The popout keeps its own fill, as the phone drawer does. Directory cards keep the prototype's 18px corners on the glass, and the other panels keep the cards' own frame radius, as the card consistency check requires off Home.

## Risks and tradeoffs

Art Gallery, Wispr and other pages that are not hardware leave the Devices menu; they stay reachable from Home's cards, the jump palette and pins. A robot's or a vacuum's own page is one more press from the menu, through its device page's tab. The Pixoo pages' headings change from page titles to the display's name, while their regions keep the page titles.
