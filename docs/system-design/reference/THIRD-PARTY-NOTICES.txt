SchemaSpy 7.0.2
https://github.com/schemaspy/schemaspy
The database directory contains generated SchemaSpy reports, including the
browser libraries, fonts and license notices supplied by its report template.
Common bower and fonts directories are shared without changing their contents.
Generated pages have a BUNNY return link, normalized dates and temporary paths,
and a viewport that allows zoom. Implied relationships and row counts are disabled.
The SchemaSpy application and SQLite JDBC driver are build tools, not shipped binaries.
Their versions, download URLs and verified checksums are recorded in tools.json.

These references describe source-owned interfaces. They do not replace the
licenses or authority of the owning controller repositories.
